# Deliverable spec — research only

> Inlined into every `research_only` post-gate worker task. Write the final
> decision-ready report to `RESEARCH.md` in the shared persistent workspace.

## Required report structure

1. **Question and approved scope**
2. **Context and evidence** — source links and relevant project materials
3. **Options considered** — including trade-offs
4. **Recommendation and rationale**
5. **Assumptions, risks, and dependencies**
6. **Likely follow-up work** — effort shape, not a precise time estimate
7. **Implementation status** — explicitly state that no software was implemented
   or deployed in this path

## Quality bar

- Distinguish verified facts from assumptions and recommendations.
- Cite external sources for factual claims.
- Do not claim implementation, testing, deployment, or external actions occurred
  unless they actually did.
