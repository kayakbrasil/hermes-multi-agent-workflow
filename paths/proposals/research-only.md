# Research-only proposal template

> The orchestrator completes this concise approval packet for a research-only
> project. Approval authorizes the defined research artifact, not implementation.

```
RESEARCH-ONLY proposal: <title>   (slug: <slug>)

Question
<the decision or uncertainty this research should resolve>

Context
- Objective: <desired outcome>
- Constraints: <known constraints>
- Initial evidence: <sources or references>

Proposed research
- Scope: <what will be investigated>
- Deliverable: <decision-ready RESEARCH.md contents>
- Excluded: implementation, deployment, and external side effects

Decision signals
- Complexity: <low | medium | high>
- Implementation effort: <small | medium | large> (research follow-up only)
- Expected external costs: <none | low | medium | high>
- Confidence: <low | medium | high>
- Major uncertainties: <bullets or none known>

Reply: approve <slug> | shelve <slug>: <reason> | modify <slug>: <change>
```
